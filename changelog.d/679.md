### Fixed
- A SIGTERM'd runner now records its KILLER, not just its death: the class
  constant keeps its verbatim prefix and gains measured fields —
  `killer_pid=… killer_cmd=… killer_uid=…` (plus the runner's `unit=…`)
  resolved from the kernel-provided sender at signal time, so an ops pass
  can separate a restart wave from a sanctioned hang-kill without log
  archaeology. Hosts where the sender-info channel is unavailable
  (non-Linux, sandboxed syscalls) keep the exact pre-32b record shape.
