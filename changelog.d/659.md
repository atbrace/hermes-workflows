fix(wf): the re-drive route guard speaks the door's alias vocabulary

The door resolves a node's bare model alias at admission through the
node's PROFILE seat (model.aliases), the tiers, and core's DIRECT_ALIASES,
and bakes the resolved target into the route receipt. The re-drive guard
expanded candidates only through the seat alias map read at the RUNNER's own
home, so a profile-routed node whose alias lives in the profile's seat died
typed route_substitution_denied on every re-drive — terminal, the node could
never retry. The door's resolver moved verbatim into the shared read model
(wfcommon.alias_provider_pair); the door delegates, and the guard resolves
the asked name through the same function, reading the seat map at the node's
route home (a new _node_route_home helper collapses three copies of the
profile-vs-runner authority split). The widening can only accept when the
resolved target equals the verified receipt; genuine substitutions stay
refused, and the literal-target identity law is unchanged.

base; the different-model (full-route zero-billing),
alias-resolves-different-target, and literal-target legs are green on both
sides. The #318 seat-config path and the #641 guard suite stay green
