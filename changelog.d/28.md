fix(wf): runtime_coordination_required becomes a typed native refusal with ONE bounded requeue

session.resume refuses runtime_coordination_required while a second writer
holds the owner session's turn lease (TTL 300 s, renewed at turn start). The
refusal used to fold into `retry/transport_error` — indistinguishable from a
socket miss — and the runner abandoned the try-later row at exit, so wakes
fired during chat activity were lost forever although the identical resume
lands minutes after lease expiry. The wake row now carries `error_reason`
verbatim; re-drives of that class inside `WAKE_REQUEUE_AFTER_S` write nothing;
the first re-drive past the window re-tries under the same Idempotency-Key; a
second refusal past the window settles the instance (`delivered=None` +
`native_refused` detail). The driver is the existing trailing-recheck marker —
no new daemon, bounded at exactly one requeue. (hw-3pb)
